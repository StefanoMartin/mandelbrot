Mandelbrot and Julia

Every point c of the plane has a Julia set of its own. Move the pointer over the first figure, or click it, to choose c; the second figure shows its Julia set.

Orbit

Point at a figure or tap it. On Fig. 1 the orbit starts at 0, with c the point; on Fig. 2 it starts at the point, with the c of the Julia set. A point belongs to the set when its orbit never escapes; the note in the corner of the figure says what happens to it.
